# 🤝 Meteor maintenance areas

Find an area you use, see who looks after it, and open its issues or pull requests. For work targeted to a release, open its [milestone](https://github.com/meteor/meteor/milestones) and filter by the area label once applied.

**Anyone can help.** Choose a report to verify or a PR to review. If you try a reproduction, describe what happened in the issue, as outlined in the [triage guide](ISSUE_TRIAGE.md#bugs). For setup, reporting bugs and submitting changes, start with the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Core Maintainers choose what they look after.** Meteor asks each Core Maintainer to give at least one area attention. You can share an area, change your focus or take a break. To opt in, add your handle to its row. A blank cell means nobody has listed their name there yet. [Core Maintainer role](GOVERNANCE.md#core-maintainer)

A maintainer may also choose a single package, a repository such as meteor/performance, or a temporary initiative such as the label migration. Record ongoing interests here. Coordinate short-term offers on the relevant issue or PR.

Core Maintainers choose which issues and PRs to advance within their areas. For major core changes, active Core Maintainers recommend a direction and the TSC makes the final decision. The TSC retains merge and official release authority. [Decision-making](GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making)

## More about Meteor OSS

- **Who holds each role?** Find Core Maintainers, the TSC, Community Package Maintainers and Alumni on the [contributors page](https://docs.meteor.com/community/contributors).
- **What is planned for a release?** See the [Meteor roadmap](https://docs.meteor.com/about/roadmap) for project priorities and the [release milestones](https://github.com/meteor/meteor/milestones) for work tracked by version.
